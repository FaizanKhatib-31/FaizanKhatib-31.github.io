Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our ingredients, roasting process, and pack sizes.

Are millet snacks healthier than regular potato chips? ▼

Yes. Unlike regular potato chips that are thinly sliced and deep-fried in palmolein oil, Root & Roasted snacks are hot-air roasted or slow-baked using whole Indian millets (jowar, ragi, and bajra). This gives you 2x to 3x more dietary fibre and plant protein, a lower glycemic index for steady energy, and up to 60% less total fat with zero trans fat.

Do your snacks contain palm oil, maida, or artificial preservatives? ▼

Never. Every Root & Roasted pouch is 100% free from palm oil, refined wheat flour (maida), maltodextrin, MSG, and artificial preservatives. Whole millet grains are always our #1 ingredient, lightly tumbled with heart-healthy rice bran or cold-pressed sunflower oil and real kitchen spices.

What is the difference between Jowar, Ragi, and Bajra snacks? ▼

Each millet brings its own distinct texture and nutritional superpower:

• Jowar (Sorghum): Pops into light, airy puffs that are naturally gluten-free, easy on digestion, and rich in soluble fibre.
• Ragi (Finger Millet): Bakes into a dense, satisfying crunch and is one of the richest plant sources of natural calcium (over 340mg per 100g).
• Bajra (Pearl Millet): Delivers a hearty, earthy crispness packed with natural iron and slow-releasing complex carbohydrates.

Which millet snack is best for weight management and calorie control? ▼

Our Peri-Peri Jowar Puffs and Himalayan Salt & Herbs Multi Millet Chips are favourites for calorie-conscious snackers. A 35g Trial Pouch contains roughly 132 kcal and over 3g of protein and fibre, keeping you full between meals without derailing your daily calorie goals.

How long do the snacks stay fresh once opened? ▼

Our 90g Core Retail Pouches feature a heavy-duty resealable zip-lock barrier lining inside our signature kraft paper exterior. Once opened, simply press the zip-lock shut to keep your puffs and crisps fresh and crunchy for up to 3 weeks without needing a separate airtight jar.

Are Root & Roasted snacks gluten-free? ▼

Yes! Jowar, ragi, bajra, and foxtail millet are naturally gluten-free ancient grains, and we never cut our batters with wheat or maida. They are safe and wholesome for anyone looking to reduce gluten in their daily diet.
